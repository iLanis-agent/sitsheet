SitSheet

Every trip starts with retyping the same instructions: the dog gets a cup at 7 and 6, the vet's number, the back door sticks. SitSheet keeps your house profile and generates a clean one-page brief for each sit - dates and sitter's name filled in, empty sections vanished, gaps flagged before you print.

  • Pets with feeding, meds, and quirks - plus the vet's number
  • House essentials: wifi, trash day, the door that sticks
  • Emergency contacts with roles ("sister, has a key")
  • Gap check: missing feeding instructions or contacts flagged before printing
  • Print-ready layout; the profile stays in your browser for next time
